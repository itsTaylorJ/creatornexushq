## What this is

An AI workspace for the boring-but-decisive parts of posting: titles,
descriptions, tags, hashtags, thumbnails, ideas, and a posting schedule. It's
aimed at every kind of creator — gaming, card openings, unboxings, vlogs,
podcasts, fitness, IRL — not just gaming.

The bit I care most about getting right: **titles and tags on YouTube should
beat what vidIQ and TubeBuddy give you.** If they don't, that's the most useful
thing you can tell me.

## What's real, and what isn't

**Real and working:**
- Titles & Descriptions, Tags & Hashtags, Thumbnails, Ideas/Hooks/CTAs, and
  Posting Schedule all generate genuine output from a real AI backend
- For YouTube, the title and tag tools pull the videos **actually ranking right
  now** for your topic and write against them. Those view counts are real.

**Not built yet, and labelled as such in the app:**
- Live Titles and Channel Audit still say "soon"
- Competitor Research, Collab Finder and Trend Tracker are deliberately gated —
  they'd have to invent channel names and fake trend data to work, so they don't
- **There is no payment of any kind.** No checkout, no card, nothing to buy.
  Pricing on the homepage is what it *will* cost after the beta.

**Nobody has live TikTok or Instagram trend data** — not us, not anyone selling
you a tool that claims otherwise. Where we don't have real data, the app says so.

## Getting started

1. Sign up with email or Google. It asks for your name, a username, and a few
   questions about your channel — that's what personalises the tools.
2. You get **50 generations a day for 7 days**, automatically. No card.
3. Every tool has a **"Try an example →"** link under its heading. Click it and
   hit generate — you'll see what the tool does in about ten seconds without
   having to think up content first.
4. Then do it again with **your own real video**. That's where you'll find out
   whether it's actually good.

## The honest quirks

Not bugs, don't report these:

- **Thumbnail scoring takes 20–30 seconds.** Vision AI is slow.
- Occasionally it'll say the image analyser is busy. Free-tier capacity. Wait a
  minute; it costs you nothing when it fails.
- The **tag score is the same every time** for the same tags — that's on purpose.
  The **thumbnail sub-scores drift a few points** between runs, because those are
  an AI opinion rather than a measurement. The app labels which is which.

## What I actually want to know

Not "it's cool" — I need the awkward stuff:

1. **Was any output good enough to genuinely use?** Which one, and did you post it?
2. **Where did it feel generic?** Generic output is the failure mode I'm most
   worried about. Name the tool and paste what it gave you.
3. **What confused you?** Anywhere you weren't sure what a field meant or what
   was about to happen.
4. **Did anything look broken on your phone?** Especially anything that made the
   page scroll sideways.
5. **Would you use this weekly?** If no — what would have to change? "No" is a
   genuinely useful answer, and it won't offend me.
6. **Compared to vidIQ or TubeBuddy** (if you've used them): better, worse, or
   not close?
